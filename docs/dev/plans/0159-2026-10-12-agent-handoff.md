verified checkpoint; external controller creates/attaches exact successor,
delivers the pointer once and gates continuation on canonical/live preflight.

messages/wakes/claims rather than copying capability or restarting their work.

## Deadline enforcement decision

The existing codex-waked loop owns continuation supervision; no per-agent daemon
or new workflow engine. Host continuation policy explicitly approves activation,
maximum polling interval and a deadline safety margin. A continuous no-dispatch
monitor verifies source remains paused and exact successor goal identity before
activation; one-shot polling cannot activate. Original deadline remains fixed.
Monitor pauses owned autonomous work at the safety boundary and interrupts only
an observed exact owned turn. Unknown effects reconcile without new goal/task
creation. Graceful monitor shutdown pauses its owned continuation; restart must
reconcile native state before another activation. A live monitor is a required
runtime acceptance condition, not a claim of enforcement during host outages.
