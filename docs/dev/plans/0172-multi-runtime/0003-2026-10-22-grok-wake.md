## Outcome

A user registers a durable wake for an exact open Grok conversation and sees that conversation receive it through the qualified MR-01 route, with truthful evidence and default idle waiting.

## Scope

Connect a built-in Grok adapter to normal scheduling and agent-facing discovery. Use native delivery when qualified, otherwise the minimal qualified terminal path needed for this outcome. Persist original intent, attempt and exact recipient; expose current method/capability.

## Non-goals

Do not substitute another session, auto-resume a dormant conversation without policy, add remote-host scope, implement unrelated Grok tools or declare lifecycle/resource parity.

## Acceptance Evidence

- [ ] AC1: deterministic trigger survives process boundaries and reaches the exact already-open Grok TUI.
- [ ] AC5: default wait, expiry, cancellation, restart and ambiguous response preserve intent and prohibit duplicate delivery.
- [ ] AC7: selected method and actual evidence are inspectable through CLI/MCP.
- [ ] Reject ambiguity, changed process/session binding and unknown composer before effects.
- [ ] Preserve the MR-02 Codex regression controls.

## Repository Surface and Risks

Grok runtime adapter, runtime-aware agent-facing wake/discovery route, durable journal and isolated acceptance.

The selected route depends on MR-01. Terminal delivery here is the minimum needed for the Grok wake; MR-05 generalizes selectable policies and fallback qualification.

A complete slice requires its stated evidence. Source completion, integration,
installed acceptance and external publication remain separate states.
