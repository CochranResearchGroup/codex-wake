## Limits and follow-up

This is opt-in, unauthenticated plain NTP. Independent agreement is operational UTC evidence under the declared trust/bounds, not cryptographic proof against a hostile network. NTS qualification failed in the preserved spike; further authentication and additional profile qualification remain follow-up work. Windows fallback is fixture-qualified and currently unavailable on this host. QPC bounds are an explicit engineering envelope, not arbitrary hardware calibration proof.

Network acquisition serializes writer transactions; competing operations wait at most30s and still fail closed on sustained contention. Older unmanaged readers are not remotely fenced. New network state is confined to the owned explicitly migrated bus and dedicated wake root; existing service roots keep legacy schemas, and no unrelated service was restarted. A package rollback alone does not downgrade schema3 or preserve later writes by restoring an old snapshot.
