## Summary

The primary checkout ordinarily stays on main. Linked worktrees remain a normal
option for off-main development, experiments, review/testing and concurrency.
Reuse each lane's existing checkout, use a predictable root and stable name,
record ownership and retention, and remove unneeded checkouts during closeout.
Tool-managed locations are supported as documented exceptions.

No runtime quota or automatic deletion is introduced. Existing unrelated
worktrees are not migrated or removed. Downstream upgrades preserve dirty
policy overlaps and stop on semantic conflicts; no downstream pushes.

Local validation on PR head 756facd: 128 selector, 49 governance and 24
harvester/rollout tests passed, plus planning, manifest, parity and diff checks.
Hosted CI was intentionally skipped under the operator's CI cost policy.
